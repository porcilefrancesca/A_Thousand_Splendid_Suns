The dataset, drawn from the OECD Gender, Institutions and Development Database and from 2023 contributions by SIGI, UNICEF, the United Nations, and the World Health Organization, provides a global overview of women's conditions and analyzes them from two perspectives: Practical realities: access to financial services, safety, and rights; and Cultural perceptions: women's perceptions regarding gender-based violence, equality, and the value of women in society.

The dataset has been refined from its original version to offer a comprehensive and objective overview of the status of women worldwide, as shown in the table below.

Refinement Details
  • Added Area, Continent, Lat/Long for geographic mapping.
  • Selected 14 specific parameters focused on objective conditions.
  • Values converted to positive percentage with a global Average.
  • Focused on female status by excluding male-only data.
