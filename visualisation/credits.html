Behind the project

We are six students from Politecnico di Milano, united by our passion for creativity, technology and innovation. As design students, we developed this project during the Computer Graphics for Information Design course. Together, we have transformed complex data into visually meaningful and engaging narratives.

Each of us brings unique skills to the table:

Francesca and Syria took care of the visual design and developed the HTML, CSS and JavaScript code for the entire website.

Arianna and Marta managed dataset processing, content research and concept development.

Giacinta and Chiara were responsible for creating the entire p5.js code, from the overall visualisation to the design of the individual suns.

A Thousand Splendid Suns // Laboratorio di Computer Grafica per l'Information Design // Scuola del Design // Politecnico di Milano // a.a. 2024/2025
